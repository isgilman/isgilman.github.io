Advances in sequencing have facilitated RNA and DNA data collection, but phenotyping — measuring any and all observable traits, such as morphology, physiology, or genomic features like gene copy number — remains a bottleneck for many areas of biology. During the course of various projects I have developed and applied novel methods to collect and analyze phenotypic data to understand the patterns and processes that shape vascular plant diversity.
