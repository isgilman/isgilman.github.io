About

Ian S. Gilman is an NSF Postdoctoral Fellow in Biology in the Department of Plant Biology at Michigan State University, working with Dr. Robert VanBuren. His research examines major evolutionary shifts in plant photosynthetic metabolism and how these shifts can serve as general models for the evolution of complex traits. He currently applies single-nuclei sequencing to study gene regulation in the genus Portulaca.
