Projects

Current and recent research projects, with funding sources and related publications.

Gene regulation of CAM & C4 photosynthesis

Applying single-nuclei sequencing to map the regulatory architecture behind integrated C4+CAM photosynthesis in Portulaca.

NSF Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Biology, Award #2208915

Related: Gilman et al. 2022, Plant Physiology; Moreno-Villena et al. 2022, Science Advances

Pelargonium CAM genome project

Investigating the genetic basis of CAM in southern African Pelargonium species to understand drought-resilience adaptations.

Department of Energy, 2025 New Investigator Portfolio

No linked publications yet.

Phylogenomics of Portullugo & beyond

Developing and applying methods for reconstructing evolutionary histories from phylogenomic and museum-specimen data, including target-enrichment and reduced-representation sequencing.

Related: Gilman et al. 2023, Annals of Botany; Lee & Gilman et al. 2021, American Journal of Botany; Martine et al. 2019, PLoS ONE

Comparative anatomy, physiology & machine learning

Using comparative and machine-learning methods to link photosynthetic anatomy, physiology, and gene expression across vascular plants.

Related: Gilman et al. 2024, New Phytologist; Marks et al. 2024, Nature Plants; Sage et al. 2023, Annals of Botany
