Ian Gilman

Plant evolutionary biology — photosynthesis, phylogenomics, and comparative genomics

I'm an NSF Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Plant Biology at Michigan State University, working with Dr. Robert VanBuren. My research examines major evolutionary shifts in plant photosynthetic metabolism — particularly C4 photosynthesis and Crassulacean acid metabolism (CAM) — and uses these shifts as general models for how complex traits evolve. My approach spans scales, from single-cell/single-nuclei sequencing of individual species to developing new phylogenomic and comparative methods. See current projects and publications for more.
