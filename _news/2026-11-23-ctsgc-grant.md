The lab received a NASA Connecticut Space Grant Consortium Faculty Project Grant for "Lowering Space Agricultural Inputs by Utilizing Crassulacean Acid Metabolism." Fairfield undergraduates will survey CAM across _Pelargonium_ and reconstruct how photosynthesis evolved in the genus, identifying closely related species to test whether CAM can cut the water and fertilizer that must be launched for space agriculture and bioregenerative life support.
